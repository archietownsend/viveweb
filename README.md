# Vive

**live longer · live better**

Vive is an iOS app that gives every meal two verdicts: an instant nutrition read on what's in it, and your body's own response, measured from your Apple Watch about two hours later.

🌐 **[vive website](https://getvive.io)**

## Features

- **The read**: point your camera at a plate, label, or menu — or just describe it — and get a clear GOOD / MIXED / AVOID verdict with full macros, judged against your diet, goals, and allergies
- **The response**: about two hours after you eat, your Apple Watch heart rate scores how your body actually handled the meal — measured, never estimated
- **Patterns**: the foods your body loves and fights, late meals that cost you sleep, and the surprises where your body disagrees with the read
- **Experiments**: one-week self-tests built from your own history — close the kitchen early, go a week UPF-free — with an honest better / worse / unclear result
- **Longevity score**: one number from diet, sleep, exercise, and cardiovascular recovery
- **Daily coach**: one focus for today, with nudges that tick off the moment you act on them
- **Sleep tracking**: a complete per-night score with stage breakdown and plain coaching
- **Zone 2**: track aerobic minutes from real workout heart-rate data
- **Recipes**: a library of healthy meals filtered to your diet, logged in one tap
